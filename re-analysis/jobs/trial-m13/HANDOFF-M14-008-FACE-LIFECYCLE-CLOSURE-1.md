## Claims and evidence

- Stock face processing consumes ordered identity changes, then enrollment count, then FaceWorld observations at 0x006551E8–0x00655274. The by-ID branch is the stock route. Native observations copy the whole incoming name at 0x004F50F8/0x004F5112–0x004F5116. Native remap likewise assigns its supplied name at 0x004F4006. Authentic empty input clears the previous name.
- TrackedFace+0x11 starts zero at 0x0087DB98 and becomes true at 0x0086CFE4–0x0086D00E when the shipped parts direction call succeeds and the three signed angles have absolute value at most 25 degrees. FaceWorld increments its total count at 0x004F47C2–0x004F47CC and the separate unsigned frontal count only on that byte at 0x004F47D2–0x004F47D8. The existing signed total greater than three or nonempty-name 0x46 gate remains at 0x004F4956–0x004F496E.
- First observation with a name captures immediate_recognition at 0x004F47EE–0x004F482E with signed face ID and $data 1. Otherwise a positive unnamed face at frontal count exactly 30 captures persistent_session_only at 0x004F48DA–0x004F4926 with $data 0, then advances the count at 0x004F494C–0x004F4952. Otherwise positive named or frontal-count-at-least-30 faces capture re_recognized at 0x004F485C–0x004F48D4 for unsigned delta at least 10001. The payload flag is 1 with a name and 0 without. Fresh insertion sets delta zero at 0x004F46CE. These events are captured before the observation report gate. Existing callback deferral determines when managed log subscribers run.
- Expiry scans unnamed entries in signed map order using the prior processed timestamp and strict unsigned comparison at 0x004F5380–0x004F53AC. After the diagnostic, the event checks positive stored ID and frontal count at least 30 on the reachable unnamed path at 0x004F53F0–0x004F5410. The signed map key and unsigned last-observed timestamp become the event value and $data at 0x004F5418–0x004F5466. This precedes RemoveFace(true) at 0x004F54A8 and its pre-erase 0x4B. Expiry checks eligibility while reserving the exact entry. A reentrant refresh after 0x4B cannot undo the erase of that entry, and the existing owner/entry fences protect replacements.
- A new remap destination starts both counters at zero, and a destination collision retains the existing entry at 0x004F4022–0x004F4036. Old removal is silent at 0x004F4070. The update_face_id event at 0x004F4074–0x004F40F2 uses signed new ID and signed old ID as $data when all three IDs are positive and the retained destination has a name or frontal count at least 30. It precedes the 0x47 common tail at 0x004F3E4E. Missing-old follows its existing path. The same-ID post-erase iterator is undefined and covered by M1-034.
- Stock recognition copies the existing narrow name and identity through 0x00863CF4 into the report. NV and file imports carry stored bytes and app assignment/rename carry UTF-8 ingress bytes. Rename compares narrow lengths and bytes at 0x00867A68–0x00867AC8 and 0x00867B54. The raw report pack at 0x007135BC calls the narrow string writer at 0x00713600, which writes the low length byte and exactly that many prefix bytes at 0x006C22DC–0x006C2326. Typed delivery retains its managed name. Standalone pack and supplied detector paths retain their established boundaries.
