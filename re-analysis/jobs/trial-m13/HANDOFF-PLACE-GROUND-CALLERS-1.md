## Native ground action

- The single retained runner uses action type 0x18, track mask 7, no interrupt and the 30-second timeout (0x554638–0x55469C, 0x52B0B3, 0x52B0C3). Init rechecks carry, saves the ID, sends 0x44 with zero pose and 100/200/500 speed/acceleration/deceleration, constructs the saved-ID/marker/max-zero verify child, and stops all motors. No carry returns 0x03000011 and failed send returns 0x03000016. Successful Init acquires trigger-8 lock on the original robot's manager (0x632A88–0x632AC4, 0x632B88–0x632BFA, 0x554794–0x5548D4, mask 0xC55A21).
- The runner waits for an observed Docking+4 busy state, then busy clear and body stationary. PickAndPlaceResult and Docking+5 do not advance it. The retained verify child owns the result, and terminal child failure clears the saved object in the current origin (0x512A96–0x512AA0, 0x5549B0–0x554A3A). Teardown releases the original reaction lock and child before generic motor/track/watcher cleanup, with no placement-specific AbortDocking (0x5546D0–0x554742). Local completion retains saved ID, count 1 and flag false. The four undefined native words remain withheld under M1-034 (0x554AE0–0x554AFA).
