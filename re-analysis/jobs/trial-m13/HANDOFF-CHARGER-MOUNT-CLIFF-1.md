## Stack changes and replay

The guide now explains the two front-sensor bits, the earlier stop packet, and the difference in timing between charger sleep and a lost link. The replay matched the existing production paths, so the change adds coverage without altering engine behavior or fidelity classifications.

- The mount replay runs from the MCP command through the native pre-dock path or the forward retry, compares the retry setup’s 0x42 bytes to the recording, and feeds the recorded C6/D4/C0 sequence. Cancellation arrives before the detailed cliff event. The retry starts from a supplied in-position pre-dock pose with rendered charger images. Firmware path IDs and timestamps are rebased to the fixture.
- The reverse replay feeds the recorded F0 wheel/gyro states through the live movement detector and gets the Back report, mount cancellation, and StopAllMotors.
- The pose replay starts at the post-path boundary using both recorded charger poses and the recorded robot pose. The stack agrees with the ARM oracle on the Y offset, threshold and result code.
- The sleep replay feeds recorded charger contacts into the engine fixture and the last actual datagram into the real offline transport. It relays the transport watchdog’s result at the engine’s disconnect boundary and sees the MCP GoingToSleep then WifiTimeout events.
