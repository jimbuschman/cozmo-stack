# FACE-RECOGNITION-STARVED-1

- Stock `vision_config.json` selects asynchronous recognition. `SetNextFaceToRecognize` (0x00865A1C..0x00865A94) requires either an enrollable face or an album entry, raw +0x30 zero, and an idle slot. It snapshots the pixels, selected PT result and admission fields. FaceTracker swaps the retained PT result at 0x0086DBAA..0x0086DBB2 and calls `GetRecognitionData` afterward at 0x0086DBC2. The native worker polls every 5 ms (0x00862D9C..0x00862DC6), marks the slot extracting, calls OKAO FR, and publishes idle on error or completed on success (0x00865034..0x008650D0). The following vision pass consumes a completed result for the queued track and looks up the current track (0x00863CF4..0x00863EF4).
- Source vision resets tracking when the head/body pose differs beyond 8 degrees or the body moves beyond the 10 mm comparison (0x006B3564..0x006B35F4). It runs face detection after that reset, and `ClearAllTrackingData` invalidates the queued track (0x00864FD8..0x00865032). The replay preserves the reset and the single result slot. No motion tolerance, admission or scoring threshold changed.
- The former stack let FR extraction take the detector lock, stalling subsequent detection and dropping camera frames while busy. FR now runs on an independent ARM guest. The copied PT validity, points and confidences are the inputs read by `OKAO_PT_GetResult` (0x00922BE0..0x00922C84), called by `OKAO_FR_ExtractHandle_GRAY` (0x0092C918..0x0092C92C). On successful extraction the shipped feature writer/reader cross the guest boundary (0x0092CED4..0x0092CF98 and 0x0092CFA0..0x0092D0C4). The vision thread keeps album access and result consumption; removal joins the worker before either guest is released. Host task dispatch and execution time depend on the host.
- With the captured enrollment-window camera, state and IMU messages replayed at 60 ms ticks into a fixture with a fresh album, the previous implementation processed 190 images and published 58 face reports. The separate extraction guest processed 303 images and published 148 face reports. Both runs assigned a positive ID, and the patched replay's first positive public report was at image timestamp 223550. This establishes the detection bottleneck, with the cause of the longer live ID delay still unconfirmed by this short replay.
- Ran both workers again from 78 s host time, before the first face ID, to 280.75 s. The previous worker processed 1,384 frames and published 52 positive-ID observations in the enrollment window, with the first at image time 223415. The independent worker processed 1,512 frames and published 130 positive-ID observations in that window, with the first at 223075. Both assigned IDs early in offline replay. The fixture replays vision inputs and the same stock recognizer, while real enrollment actions and device load were exercised in the separate integration test. The exact reason for the live 27-second span of anonymous observations and the original phone's absolute timing remain unconfirmed.
- The retained enrollment integration restores the synthetic named album, drives the stock detector and recognizer through four actual enrollment samples and observes phase 4 and the new name. The one-shot test observes the count update whether the result is already consumed or still in the slot. 78 focused tests, two before/after replay pairs, fidelity and the commands dry-run passed. No record was reclassified.

Full-suite follow-up. The positive-merge regression assumed that a completed recognition slot would still be visible after each camera frame. A frame can consume its own result. The fixture now waits for the actual ID, enrollment sample and completion states, retaining the report-order assertions. The previously failing test passed in isolation after the correction.
