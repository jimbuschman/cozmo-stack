## Why this job

The CM13-01 fixture race showed that the stack's lattice planner keeps references to goal `Pose3d` objects that the
caller owns. `NativeLatticePlannerRuntime.ComputePath` stores `goals.ToArray()`, which is a new array holding the same
Pose3d objects, and the worker reads them later through `ToStateC`. If the caller disposes a goal, or the goal changes
while planning runs, the stack can plan to the wrong place or fault. Whether the engine copies the goals and the
start state, and when, has not been read. This is the open part of:

- M13-033 IMPLEMENTATION_GAP, the planner side of PathComponent;
- M11-084 RECOVERABLE_GAP, pose wrapper lifetimes in production. Its unresolved text keeps the goal-copy window
  before 0x00558FB4 and the planner thread's indirect pose calls open.

Quote both records' current title, status and unresolved text from the manifest before relying on them.
