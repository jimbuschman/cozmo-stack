## Rules for every log line

Every engine log the rows name is reproduced exactly:
- the level;
- the channel (channeled calls only);
- the key;
- the format and every argument, in order and with the printf conversions (%d signed, %u unsigned; %f-family under
  M4-032);
- the gate;
- the errG/break tail, where present (the stack's equivalent of `_errG`; check how earlier rounds represent it);
- its position relative to sends, stores and broadcasts.

Expected values in tests come from the rows (the strings and the orders), never from the C#.
