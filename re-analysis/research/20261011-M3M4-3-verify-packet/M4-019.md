> ### 13.4 M4-017: state entries that are not objects
>
> DefineFromJson's `operator[](char const*) const` and isMember both call find(). find() throws Json::LogicError ("in
> Json::Value::find(key, end, found): requires objectValue or nullValue") for any type other than null or object.
>
> - A non-object root (one that passed empty()) throws at root["charging"], **after** "OffCharger" was inserted.
> - A non-object state value (array, string, number, bool, or `[]`) throws at its first isMember, **before** InvalidJson.
