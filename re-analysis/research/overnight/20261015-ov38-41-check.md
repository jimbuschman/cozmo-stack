# Opus check of OV38–OV41 (M9-013, M9-014, M9-024, M9-025), 2026-10-15

- **Checked:** `research/overnight/20261014-ov38-m9-013.md`, `-ov39-m9-014.md`, `-ov40-m9-024.md` and
  `-ov41-m9-025.md` (Codex), against libcozmoEngine.so (the Wwise runtime) and all six shipped banks. Read-only
  cozmo-extractor.
- **Adopted:** by the manager on 2026-10-15.
- **Result:** all four answers hold on every behaviour-changing row that was re-read, and no address is fabricated.
- **Gaps in them:**
  - OV40 misses the MIDI note-off release path (0x00A3EA3C → 0x00A3E920).
  - OV41 doesn't name byte +0x44, which chooses scalar or buffered output.
  - OV38's twelve pitch-bounded nodes are RanSeq containers, not Sounds.

## Manager decisions

1. **M9-013 and M9-014 stay RECOVERABLE_GAP** until a follow-up Opus pass re-checks the rows this check didn't
   re-disassemble:
   - OV38's E1–E12;
   - OV39's G1–G3 and G8;
   - the source-context consumers.

   M9-013's effect text is wrong: audibility depends on selection and the 12 single-pitch RanSeq gates (M9-022).
2. **M9-024 splits:**
   - **(a) The envelope stop mechanics move to IMPLEMENTATION_GAP.**
   - **(b) The envelope initializer and timing** become a new RECOVERABLE_GAP record.
   - **(c) The MIDI note-off voice-kind dispatch** becomes a new RECOVERABLE_GAP record. Its rows are read, but what
     PBI+0x38 does isn't.
   - **Not singing-only:** six vo_external envelopes (selector 1, policy 1) reach the same stop path.
3. **M9-025 moves to IMPLEMENTATION_GAP** for the mode-0 path.
   - **New RECOVERABLE_GAP records:** the writer of modulator byte +0x44 (which chooses scalar or buffered), and the
     engine sample-rate and frame-count globals behind the fixed-48000 step.
   - **Not queued for build** until both are read, because the shipped vibrato's output depends on them.
   - **Modes 1–4** are reached by shipped content only through curves that map to 0 cents, so they have no audible
     effect. They're recorded, not built.
4. **The ADP-1 boundary for the LFO:** the LFO output is a modulation value, not PCM.
   - **Exact:** its shape, mode, phase, the 48000 base, the scalar/buffered choice, the region scheduling, the ramp, the
     publication gate, and the polynomial constants with their f32 operation order.
   - **May be EQUIVALENT_IMPLEMENTATION:** only the libm rounding in the smoothing coefficient (not reached by shipped
     content), and the per-sample application of pitch inside the resampler.
5. **M9-012 (EXACT_SOURCE) gets a lead, not a contradiction.** The runtime note-tracking flag may be NodeBase +0x47 bits
   2–3, not property 45, and 15 Sounds outside the singing closure set the authored bit 0x10. The follow-up pass checks
   it.

  2 Layers, the target, and ActorMixer 62050212.
- **4 LFOs and 7 envelopes, all in Cozmo.bnk.**
  - Only 528935089 (cozmo_singing_vibrato_lfo) maps to a non-zero curve: pitch, (0, 0) → (1, 580), linear.
  - The other three LFOs map to 0.
  - No LFO uses smoothing (property 6).
- **No binding to velocity source 0x81 was found.**
- **The voice kinds:**
  - get-in 403781184 is kind 0: note-off doesn't stop it;
  - 462443456 (flags 0x24) is kind 2: a loop break at note-off, plus the note-off envelope 381606890;
  - 774902407 (property 0x2E = 2) is kind 1: play-on-note-off.

## Confirmed rows

- **M9-013:**
  - the Layer fan-out, 0x009D0758..0x009D0BCC (N+0x84; the filter per child; the loop continues on a rejection);
  - the MIDI filter, 0x009EE454..0x009EE940 (channel mask 0x35, note offset ±0.5 then `vcvt.s32`, `usat #7`, velocity
    clamped to 1..127, ranges 0x31–0x34, write-back on success);

  - the play slot 0x00A0AFDC.
- **M9-014:**

- **M9-024 (b), timing (unread):** S+0x38 = parameter+8, from the initializer 0x009D5934..0x009D671C (sustain property 13
