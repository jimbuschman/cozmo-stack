**Corrections to M11-084's unresolved text:**
- **"Unreachable: RemoveOwner.ZeroOwners; ZeroOwnersWithID outside ~PoseOriginList"** holds only without the D2 race.
  They are HARDWARE_ONLY (M11-095).
- **(a) is partly contradicted:**
  - UnSetCarryingObjects never re-parents;
  - the erase order doesn't matter, because DeleteLocatedObjects copies each pose first;
  - T's PoseConfirmation record is a third holder.
- **Copies carry the parent shared_ptr,** and operator= skips the UnownedParent VERIFY.

**Still unread:**
