## Corrections

- **The writer is not /sqrt(3)** (0x0050DAA4..0x0050DB0E):
  1. Take the translation and `vabs` x, y, z.
  2. s = |y| < |z| ? |z| : |y|; then s = |x| < s ? s : |x|. Both compares are `vcmpe` + `it mi`, so an unordered compare
     keeps the left operand.
  3. `vmul.f32` by **0x3F13CD3A**, then store at +0x14.

  The stored value is ordered selection then a multiply. It can round differently from a divide by sqrt(3).
- **M12-038's authority** says "no reader … in IDockAction::GetPreActionPoses' compaction". In fact the compaction
  **copies** +0x14 (0x00550B52, 0x00550B96), and so does GetCurrentPreActionPoses (an inlined assignment,
  0x004DFBEA..0x004DFC04). Neither uses it in a decision.
- **GetParent** is 0x008453D4..0x0084541E (the Codex range was wrong). The root fault is at 0x008452BA (M11-076).
