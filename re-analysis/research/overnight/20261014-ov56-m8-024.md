## Flags and destruction

At entry to ~Robot, values are instance state, not a teardown-wide triplet. AbortAll contains no write to these three bytes and no IBehavior::Stop call. A prior Stop sets A1=0 and suppresses the callback; a prior cancellation request can set A0=1 and reject successor StartActing; an ordinary successful Init/Resume leaves the gates open. Resume's A2 is temporary around its virtual call, not a shutdown bit.
