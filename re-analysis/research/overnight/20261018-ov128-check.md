- **O1.** SetLights' third argument is ObjectLights+0x70 (0x00637EEC), sent as CubeID's second byte: 0xFF when 0xFFFFFFFF,
  else u32 floor((v+29)/30) (`umull` 0x88888889, `>>4`, 0x0063A7A2..0x0063A7BC).
- **O2.** HandleImageChunk does nothing before time sync: robot+0x29 == 0 skips AddChunk (0x00535A7A..0x00535A80). It also
  forwards ImageChunk to the game depending on robot+0x340/+0x344 (0x00535AA2..0x00535AFE): a lead for its owner.
- **O3.** Pending is cleared after each process, so a frame completing during processing is dropped (allocation kept).
- **O4.** AddChunk rejects chunks over 0x4B0 (0x004F1CEA..0x004F1CF4), out-of-order ids clear valid (0x004F1DB2..0x004F1DF4),
  a count mismatch on the last chunk clears it (0x004F1E1E..0x004F1E22); invalid → no SetNextImage (0x004F1EAE..0x004F1EC6).
  Radio loss cannot produce a short raw payload; only a firmware frame with too few bytes can.
- **O5.** VisionSystem.cs:957 and FaceActions.cs:127 use `History.At` (nearest, later on tie) as the frame pose.
- **O6.** RobotStateHistory.cs:142 says "different origin ids" for 0x005314BA; they are frame IDs (queued).

## M3-037

A short raw payload is decoded from bytes [n, N) of the receiver's allocation (capacity ≥ 230400; N = 76800 gray, 230400
RGB), one of three recycled slots (I4, I5, O3). Bytes written by earlier frames depend on exchange/drop history and
scheduling; never-written bytes are indeterminate; in C++ terms reading past `size()` is undefined. Zero-fill invents pixel
content feeding marker and face detection. This falls under the M1-034 standing rule: skip the frame's decode, keep the
prior image state, log visibly naming M1-034 and M3-037. Long-payload truncation stays exact. Reachability (firmware
emitting encodings 1/2 with short totals) is UNKNOWN: settle through the firmware image or a hardware capture over every
ImageRequest mode/resolution the engine can send, logging encoding and total length per completed image.
