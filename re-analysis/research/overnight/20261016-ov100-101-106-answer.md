## M11-084: pose wrapper lifetimes (stays RECOVERABLE_GAP, narrowed)

**Unreachable:**
- ZeroOwners;
- ZeroOwnersWithID outside ~PoseOriginList;
- AssignmentOperator.NotLastOwner: no null-node wrapper ever exists, because PoseBase() at 0x00845724 has no callers;
- SetParent.UnownedParent for live wrappers;
- SanityCheckOwnership (no callers).

**Destructor.NotLastOwner and the UnownedParent Dev_Asserts** fire exactly when a node's last wrapper dies while a child
node or a shared_ptr still refers to it.

**All 35 SetParent sites are classified.**

**Open:**
- **(a)** SetObjectAsAttachedToLift (0x00633010) parents the object on top to the carried object's pose. Whether
  NotLastOwner fires depends on the DeleteLocatedObjects erase order (0x0061D0A6..0x0061D602) and UnSetCarryingObjects
  (0x00633C00).
- **(b)** GetWithRespectTo callers that pass a temporary `to`.
- **(c)** Copies that share a parent and outlive it.
- **(d)** Vision-thread poses updating the owner count non-atomically.
