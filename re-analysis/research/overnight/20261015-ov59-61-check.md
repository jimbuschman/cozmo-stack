# Opus check of OV59, OV60 and OV61 (M15-050, M15-051, M11-085), 2026-10-15

- **Checked:** `research/overnight/20261015-ov59-m15-050.md`, `-ov60-m15-051.md` and `-ov61-m11-085.md` (Codex, a fresh
  fast context), against libcozmoEngine.so and the shipped config. Read-only cozmo-extractor.
- **Adopted:** by the manager on 2026-10-15.
- **Result:**
  - **OV59** is confirmed, with no errors.
  - **OV60** has three contradictions and an incomplete Update chain.
  - **OV61** is confirmed. Its pool rows belong to M4-010.

## Manager decisions

1. **M15-050 moves to IMPLEMENTATION_GAP** (job B-M15-5).
   - **When it writes:** not only at connection, but also on every app resume (SetGameBeingPaused(false), 0x006990DA).
   - **The NV padded overread:** the original copies round4(size) bytes, which reads 0–3 bytes past the Lab vector and
     sends them on the wire (0x0064461C..0x00644632, 0x0064472E..0x00644734).
     - **It isn't a lost unit,** so "skip the smallest unit" would wrongly drop the write.
     - **The stack sends those pad bytes as 0,** logged once on the stack channel. That's the same ruling as the
       never-written inventory words (M15-035; operator-confirmed on 2026-10-15).
     - **M3-043 owns it.** M3-043's cross-reference to M3-037 is wrong: M3-037 covers only short image payloads.
2. **SetGameBeingPaused is an app input.** The stack supplies it from a host pause/resume event (COMPATIBILITY_POLICY,
   app-boundary rule), and M15-016 owns building it. Without it, the resume writeback, ApplyDecay and the Needs resume
   work never run.
3. **M15-051 is split.**
   - **(a) M15-051 moves to IMPLEMENTATION_GAP:**
     - the phase;
     - the notify;
     - the group;
     - the assignments and getters;
     - Update and the stage;
     - UpdateMusic;
     - the BehaviorManager writers, including all four setter callers;
     - the lookups.

     **Its evidence is corrected:** UpdateChooserPhase is at 0x005A7A54; 0x005A8854 is GetDesiredActiveBehaviorInternal.
   - **(b) A new IMPLEMENTATION_GAP record:** ActivityBuildPyramid's behaviour choice, with OV60's three contradictions
     corrected.
   - **(c) A new RECOVERABLE_GAP record:** the Update forwarding hop through ActivityFreeplay (0x005AD9D0..0x005AD9DE) and
     ActivitySparked (0x005B186C..0x005B18D0), plus ActivitySparked's spark-state logic, which has no other owner.
   - **Writes to +0x58..+0x65 through other pointers** stay UNKNOWN. The bounded census found only the four direct writers.
     That's accepted as noted; it isn't blocking.
4. **M11-085 moves to IMPLEMENTATION_GAP,** owning only the tail (0x00533CA4..0x00533D32).
   - **The pool rows go to M4-010's CD7.** It gains the factory-ID mismatch branch and both Info logs.
   - **Inventory row LC8a** (M4-control.md) gains the returned-ID ≠ −1 gate.
   - **A new RECOVERABLE_GAP record:** the GuardDog and Feeding disconnect consumers.
