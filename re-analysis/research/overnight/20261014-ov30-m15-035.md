## Initialized state versus undefined memory

The distinction is observable and must not be hidden by a zero-filled managed array. Allocation 0x0051014E does not promise zeroed heap memory. Constructor 0x0063C928 writes two words, because the named InventoryType domain has `Invalid=0`, `Sparks=1` (EnumToString 0x0077D3B0..D3C6); it does not initialize the 62 reserved words of the fixed 64-word message. A successful 256-byte-or-longer read overwrites all 64 words and removes that uncertainty. A later short read after such a restoration has defined retained suffix values and must not be rejected or zero-padded.

On the first missing/error/short callback, any never-written words remain indeterminate.
