The native conclusion is conditional but positive: **a previously instantiated
Hijack bus slot can remain owned by its bus across voice connection destruction,
and the subsequent Term bus loop invokes it**. Voice count becoming0 does not
itself destroy that bus/slot. An intervening earlier sweep, explicit bank/FX
replacement, Init failure, or no robot audio can make the slot absent; no claim
is made that every shutdown invokes this callback. The supplied engine code gives
no requirement to postpone shutdown until all buses have been swept away.

The shipped names `Robot_Bus_1` ID2678428988 and custom Hijack ID412442143 are
listed in `re-analysis/obb/sound_meta/Init.txt:126` and`:137`; prior extraction of
the bank's slot3 association is a navigation lead (M6-013), not newly revalidated
bank parsing here. This answer's positive lifetime statement starts from a
successfully instantiated slot; registry success and environmental playback are
conditions, not assumed universal success. A specific recorded shutdown's exact
bus/FX population is **UNKNOWN** without that run's state/capture.
