# Opus check of OV65, OV66 and OV67 (M6-032, M6-033, M15-006 ActivateSpark), 2026-10-15

- **Checked:** `research/overnight/20261015-ov65-m6-032.md`, `-ov66-m6-033.md` and `-ov67-activatespark.md` (Codex, a
  fresh fast context), against libcozmoEngine.so (ARM for Wwise, Thumb for Anki) and the Unity scripts (authority 2).
  Read-only cozmo-extractor. All three hold on every row that was re-read.
- **Adopted:** by the manager on 2026-10-15.

## Manager decisions

1. **M6-032 moves to IMPLEMENTATION_GAP.**
   - **While the map is live:** the destroy and process lambdas behave exactly as the original: the Info logs, the
     find, then CloseAudioBuffer/UpdateBuffer, or the NotFound Info.
   - **After the map dies:** each lambda's find faults at 0x00595E08, because the bucket pointer is 0 and the bucket
     count is still non-zero once any Robot existed. So both lambdas are M1-034 skips under the standing rule, logged
     visibly:
     - destroy: 0x00596030 → 0x00595E08, after its Info at 0x00596002;
     - process: 0x0059615A → 0x00595E08.
   - **Whether the crash actually happens** at shutdown depends on audio activity and thread timing, so that part is
     HARDWARE_ONLY.
   - **A new RECOVERABLE_GAP:** what moves a bus's +0x1BC from 1 back to 4 (the callers of 0x00A4F36C, 0x00A4F544 and
     their siblings).
2. **M6-033 is split.**
   - **IMPLEMENTATION_GAP:** the hook, package, registration, device-dispatch and worker-stop rows. The M1-034 points are
     0x008D9914 and 0x008D8062.
   - **A new RECOVERABLE_GAP:** the device storage cleanup (0x0096D624..0x0096FE6F, 0x00969DBC, 0x0097CC20), stream vt+8 at
     0x008D9E54, and the allocator bodies.
   - **M6-031 gains an M1-034 point:** the list-invariant trap at 0x009EAFD4..0x009EAFDC.
3. **ActivateSpark is split into new records.**
   - **IMPLEMENTATION_GAP:**
     - (1) the G2E 0x96 handler with its four cases, plus RequestATrick;
     - (2) the spark fields, the setter, the switch and the high-level-activity adapter clear;
     - (3) the ActivitySparked lifecycle, including its E2G replies HardSparkStartedByEngine (tag 131) and
       HardSparkEndedByEngine (tag 132).
   - **M15-006 keeps** the Freeplay pick and switch rows.
   - **(4) The app inputs** are an operator question (below).
   - **M15-055** (the forwarding hop and spark state from the OV59–61 check) now has its spark-state rows read here.
     Those rows are owned by record (3); M15-055 keeps only the forwarding hop.

## OV67: ActivateSpark

**The app side (authority 2):**
- **The outer message:** G2E tag 0x96, BehaviorManagerMessage, with a one-byte union tag:

  | Union tag | Message | Payload / sender |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | 0 | ActivateSpark | int32 UnlockId |
  | 1 | SparkUnlocked | no Unity sender |
  | 2 | DoATrickRequest | empty; DoRandomSpark |
  | 3 | PlayAGameRequest | empty; RequestRandomGame |

- **Cancelling** is ActivateSpark(Count = 0x55).
- **Senders:** SparksDetailModal, CozmoSingsDetailModal and OnboardingDoTrickStage.
- **The app deducts the spark cost itself** for ActivateSpark.

**The build rows:**
- **The subscription** (0x005A15AC..0x005A15CE; adapter 0x005A5136..0x005A514A).
- **The dispatch:** a tag > 3 is an error; tbb 02 9C 12 A7 (0x005A3C70..0x005A3C78).
- **Case 0:** SetRequestedSpark(id, false). If id == 0x55, set +0x65 = 1 (0x005A3C80..0x005A3C9A).
- **Case 1:** SetRequestedSpark(id, true) (0x005A3DB4..0x005A3DC6).
- **Case 2** (0x005A3CA0..0x005A3D5A; 0x0077E0CC..0x0077E0DE):
  1. RequestATrick;
  2. a cost of 3;
  3. AddInventoryAmount(1, −3), unconditionally;
  4. the sEvent "meta.spark_random_trick" if +0x60 ≠ 0.
- **RequestATrick** (0x0056E6D0..0x0056E838): cumulative weights; redraw while the pick is 0 or the previous pick; stop at
  1000 and log an Error.
- **Case 3:** +0x38 = 1 when +0x30 is null or +0xA1 is set (0x005A3DCA..0x005A3DD6).
- **A bad tag:** sErrorF "BehaviorManager.HandleEvent.UnhandledMessageUnionTag" (0x005A3D5C..0x005A3DAE).
- **The setter** (0x005A3EAC..0x005A3F0E).
- **The fields' init** (0x005A08DA..0x005A0900).
- **The writers** (ctor; 0x005A3C9A; 0x005A3EB2/0x005A3EB6; 0x005A4220/0x005A4224/0x005A4226; 0x005A4FDC/0x005A4FDE).
- **The adapter:** SetCurrentActivity, then clear when v ≠ 1 (0x005A4FA2..0x005A4FE0). SetCurrentActivity's body 0x005A106C
  is unread, and overlaps M15-031.
- **The switch:** the Info comes first; +0x60 isn't cleared (0x005A4198..0x005A422C).
- **The Freeplay pick** (M15-006, 0x005AE29C..0x005AE762).
- **Sparked OnSelected,** with HardSparkStartedByEngine (0x005B1518..0x005B16DC; 0x005B171C).
- **Sparked OnDeselected** ends with CubeLightComponent::StopAllAnims (0x005B1780..0x005B179E). OV67 omitted that.
- **The objective and the observed set** (0x005B181E..0x005B1866).
- **Update, the checks, the end and the interrupt** (0x005B186C..0x005B1A16).
- **Get-in, get-out and internal** (0x005B1D18..0x005B1F2A; hard max 0x005B1F94..0x005B2066).
- **Complete,** with the meta.upgrade_replay_* events and HardSparkEndedByEngine (0x005B1A90..0x005B1C2E; 0x005B2094).
- **Unread:** ResetLightsAndAnimations 0x005B17A2..0x005B181D, the ReloadFromConfig exception tail 0x005B11AA..0x005B1255,
  and the RandInt and IsUnlocked bodies.
