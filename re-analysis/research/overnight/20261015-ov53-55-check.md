# Opus check of OV53, OV54 and OV55 (M11-081, M11-082, M8-023), 2026-10-15

- **Checked:** `research/overnight/20261014-ov53-m11-081.md`, `-ov54-m11-082.md` and `-ov55-m8-023.md` (Codex, a fresh fast
  context), against libcozmoEngine.so and the shipped config. Read-only cozmo-extractor.
- **Adopted:** by the manager on 2026-10-15.
- **Result:** none is contradicted on substance. OV55 has one citation slip. All three leave things out, mostly
  reachability, and the binary settles three of OV55's capture gaps.

## Manager decisions

1. **M11-081 moves to IMPLEMENTATION_GAP** (job B-M11-11).
   - **Unreachable:** the vector overload (0x0085FAE4) has no caller.
   - **No ADP-1 question:** `roundf` is exactly specified IEEE, and ADP-1 doesn't cover M11.
2. **M11-082 moves to IMPLEMENTATION_GAP:** the sorted EnableMode order, and the defined failure tail (lower-level logs,
   then the loader error, then continue, with no clear).
   - **The shipped vision_config.json parses,** so the failure tail and the partial-object contents can't be reached with
     shipped assets. The partial-object contents are recorded as unreachable. They aren't built and no RECOVERABLE_GAP is
     opened.
   - **The getMemberNames and CZString throws** come under the M1-034 skip.
3. **M8-023 moves to IMPLEMENTATION_GAP:** the confirmed destructor bodies and orders, plus closures 1–3.
   - **Closure 3** (FactoryTest +0x180 is always empty) rests on an offset scan. It's accepted, because FactoryTest can't
     run in the shipped app outside SDK (M7-027/M7-022 reasoning), so nothing observable depends on it.
   - **CloseLog fires only if StartLog ran,** and that never happens in the shipped app.
   - **EraseVisualization's VizManager traffic is dead:** SendVizMessage is `bx lr` (0x006C05EC, the OV42–44 check).
4. **The IBehavior +0x88 capture census** goes into M8-024's census, which already has the +0x88 lead. No new record.
5. **New RECOVERABLE_GAP:** the ProtoHandle subscriber-callable census.
