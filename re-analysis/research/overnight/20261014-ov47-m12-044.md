## Layout and what counts as an escape

Use E for a 28-byte PreActionPose element. The established layout is action type E+0, marker E+4, embedded 12-byte Pose3d wrapper E+8..E+0x13, tolerance E+0x14, other float E+0x18. Passing E+8 to a pose function does not pass the tolerance field. A copy of that wrapper is likewise not a PreActionPose copy. Vector-header arguments are included here because their callees can index E; allocation release is listed separately rather than silently omitted.

No listed consumer passes E or a reference to E to a new semantic callee. The only whole-container semantic escape after production is Init's virtual GetDockMarker2. Its concrete targets are closed below.
