## Corrections

- **M12-039's authority** names Rotation3d::GetAngleAround{X,Y}axis, which is wrong. The PLTs 0x004AB914 and 0x004AB908
  are **RotationMatrix3d**'s (bodies 0x0084B370 and 0x0084B3CA). M12-030's MISSING note names the wrong class in the same
  way.
- **M12-039's effect** is imprecise. The calculation runs only when useExactRotation (+0x178) is set. Mismatched up axes
  fail with 0x0300000B; they don't pick an angle.

## Reachability (Unity, authority 2)

dev behaviour. Under the app-boundary rule the engine side stays exact, and that input is the stack API's to supply.
