**Why (a) is not built:**
- **MatPiece (FlatMat, Bridge, Platform):** no instance is ever constructed. Every direct call to their ctors comes from
  their own CloneType, and their VTT/vtable GOT entries are referenced only by their ctors and dtors.
- **CustomObject:** the factories are called only from BlockWorld's handlers for DefineCustomBox, DefineCustomCube,
  DefineCustomWall and CreateFixedCustomObject, and from the dev behaviour DockingTestSimple. No Unity code sends those
  messages, and DockingTestSimple is in no shipped config. Under the app-boundary rule they are SDK-only, and **out of
  scope**.
- **The read rows stay here,** in case that changes:
  - MatPiece: M1–M3, F1–F4, and the base-object variant 0x004F6B58..0x004F6C94;
  - CustomObject: U1–U4, plus the clone 0x004F0470..0x004F04DC, which re-runs the ctor.

## Build rows (part c)

- **S1–S6, SortCornersClockwise:**
  - **the centroid** (0x004DF7BE..0x004DF84C): c = P0, then +P2, +P1, +P3, then ×0.25f, in f32;
  - **the angles** (0x004E7EFC..0x004E7F9C): atan2f(dy, dx) in f32, per corner;
  - **the sort:**
    - n = 4 goes to libc++ __sort4 (0x004E833A, the tbb at 0x004E847E, 0x004E84D4..0x004E84E4);
    - sort3 (0x004E8508..0x004E85BC): every test is `vcmpe` + `bpl` (false on equal or unordered);
    - so finite equal keys keep their input order, and NaN follows the literal branch tree;
  - **the result:** Quadrilateral(p[asc0], p[asc3], p[asc1], p[asc2]) (0x004E7F7A..0x004E7F96). The ctor
    (0x004E7E9C..0x004E7EFA) does a memclr, then integer word copies, so NaN payloads and the sign of zero are kept.
- **R1–R3, the Rectangle fallback:**
  - **R1, empty input:** four zero words (0x004E81B4..0x004E81C6).
  - **R2, the bounds** (0x004E81F6..0x004E825A):
    - min x and y are replaced on `gt`, max y and x on `mi`, all carried as integer bits;
    - width = maxX − minX and height = maxY − minY, in f32.
  - **R3, the result** (0x004E676A..0x004E67C4): center = (x + w·0.5, y + h·0.5) in f32, size = (w, h), angle = 0.
    Then `__cxa_end_catch`, then the RotatedRect::points path (0x004E6512).
  - The body is 0x004E81B4. 0x004E6778 is a call site.
