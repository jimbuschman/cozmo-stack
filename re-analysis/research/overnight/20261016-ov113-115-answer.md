- **Method:** a read-only Opus cozmo-extractor, against libcozmoEngine.so (ARM, literals at pc+8, capstone with
  skipdata) and the shipped banks, parsed locally. All 604 music objects and 2886 hierarchy objects are consumed
  exactly. Scripts are in the session scratchpad `r113\` (mus.py, aud.py, census.py, hdr.py, sim.py, sing.py, vorb.py,
  modr.py).
- **Adopted:** by the manager on 2026-10-16. An Opus answer is the checked evidence; no second pass.

**M1-034 points:**
- **0x00A4B194..0x00A4B19C:** a connection key not in the device list leads to a null dereference and `udf`.
- **0x009D29D4..0x009D29DC:** `udf` when an entry is in no pool block (unreachable, F3).
- **0x009E6BB8 → 0x009E69CC:** a scaling-3 curve uses an undefined register. Unreachable: every shipped param 0/7
  curve uses scaling 2.

**Manager rulings:**
- **Q1, ADP-1:** the 9E6810 base and scale, the 9E84C8 factor and the A4AF50 levels are parameter values computed per
  voice and connection, and A4AF50 feeds a threshold decision. They are **exact** (fast log and pow10 reproduced
  bit-for-bit), consistent with the M6-040 ruling. Only the per-sample application of the resulting gain is ADP-1.
- **Q2–Q5:** AB22D4 readiness, the B1 re-attach question, the consumer of out+0x58 and game-object aux sends, A35998
  and the transition functions, and the continuous getin path (H1) all go into the new RECOVERABLE_GAP record M9-046.
- **C4 lead (M9-038):** per-frame modulator pushes skip params 0 and 7.
