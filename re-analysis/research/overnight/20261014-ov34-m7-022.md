## Random arithmetic and exact consumption

`RandDblInRange(a,b)` at `0x0082FA48..72` computes in this order, all binary64: `width=b-a`, call GetNextDbl, multiply `width*u`, add `a`. GetNextDbl (`0x0082F9B0..0x0082FA12`) consumes two MT19937 u32 outputs, first L then H; converts H to f64, multiplies by `2^32`, converts L to f64, adds L, multiplies by `2^-64`, then applies its stored distribution endpoints: `(upper-lower)*value+lower`, with separate f64 operations. Constants at `82FA18/20`. Constructor `82F7F8..816` initializes those endpoints to 0.0 and 1.0. Preserve these operations and their rounding, rather than treating the mathematical interval as proof of an excluded upper endpoint: the u64-to-f64 construction can round its largest values to 1.0. The MT state and engine seed/current shared-generator position must be supplied by the owning RNG path; no new independent test-local RNG is established here.
