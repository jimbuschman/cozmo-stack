**A8-15's reachability:**
- T must be known to be on C when the pick-up succeeds. Whether shipped flows dock to a C with a known T on top is
  unread: the consumer of IDockAction+0xC0 and CanInteractWithObjectHelper.
- An origin change must happen while carrying.

**A8-16:** UpdatePoseOfStackedObjects parents T to **C's parent**, not to C (0x006219A0..0x006219A8).

**A8-17:** UpdatePoseInInstance, SeeingCarriedObject (0x00505F5A..0x00505FBC):
- re-poses a carried object and calls UnSetCarryObject, which clears both carried IDs while T stays parented to C;
- whether a carried object is routed there is unread.

**A8-18:** DeleteLocatedObjects clears, clones and copies only objects in the **current** origin. Objects in other
origins are erased bare (0x0061D1C6).

## B3, (c) and (d)

- **Harmless while running:** `to` owned by the robot pose, the origins, the lift pose, the camera pose, or the new
  origin.
- **Harmless:** 0x0062196E.
- **0x00632FB6 is A1:** harmful exactly on A8-15 and A8-17.

**(c):**
- ~ObservableObject destroys the markers before the pose (0x004E0D78..0x004E0DA0).
- DriveToPoseAction::Init re-parents each goal to the world origin in place (0x0055A8A6..0x0055A91E). The window
  before Init is unread (0x00558FB4 and the queue Init timing).
- The concrete ActiveCube/Block destructors are unread.

**(d):**
- The LatticePlannerImpl worker (0x005007E0) and FaceRecognizer::Run (0x00862D70) make no direct pose-tree calls.
- Indirect calls are unread.
- So M11-095 stays limited to the vision thread.
