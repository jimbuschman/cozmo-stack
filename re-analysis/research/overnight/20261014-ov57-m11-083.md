## Decimal algorithm, independent of library labels

Use a signed output-prefix flag and the absolute input. Define `r` as the radix limb, `a` as first occupied limb, `z` as one-past-last. The native steps read above produce these operations:

1. `y = 2*frexp(abs(value), &e2)`; if nonzero, decrement e2. For nonzero y, multiply y by 2^28 and decrement e2 by28.
2. Fill base-10^9 limbs with `v=(uint32)y; y=1e9*(y-v)` until y is zero. Placement leaves room on either side of r.
3. For e2>0, repeatedly shift the limbs left by min(e2,29), using uint64 division/remainder by1e9 and propagating carry toward a. For e2<0, repeatedly shift right by min(-e2,9); carry to the next limb is `(1e9>>shift)*(limb & ((1<<shift)-1))`.
4. The right-shift retention limit is `p/9+2`. **For f the base is r** (compare conversion to0x66 at 0x00081A14); for g it is a. With p6 this retains two limbs past the radix bound, rather than two relative to the most significant occupied limb. This is a behavior-changing difference from FormatG.
5. Set j=p for f. Locate the rounding limb using the signed division expression based on `(j+9216)/9-1024`; divisor is the power of10 selected by the within-limb index. With p6, this is the first fractional limb divided by1000. Inspect discarded remainder and whether more limbs remain. No decimal string or locale grouping is involved in this step.
6. The rounding probe is `round=2^53 + (quotient_is_odd ? 2 : 0)`. `small=.5` below half, `1` at exact half with no later limb, `1.5` otherwise. For negative output negate both. Subtract the discarded residue from the limb. If `round+small != round`, add the decimal divisor and propagate base1e9 carry. This addition uses the ambient FPSCR. Under round-to-nearest it gives tie-to-even for the tested f6 positions; it is not a hard-coded floor(x+.5) or away-from-zero algorithm.
7. Clamp z to the rounding boundary and strip zero tail as the native loop does. f bypasses g's significant-digit selection and trailing-zero reduction. Emit all integer positions (at least `0`), `.`, precisely six fractional positions including padding, and sign. Very large finite inputs remain fixed decimal; no `e+` conversion.

The binary decomposition and integer limb arithmetic are exact operations; the algorithm deliberately truncates its retained expansion and uses floating-point rounding probes. It should be reproduced as these steps, rather than labeled globally correctly rounded based on a few outputs. The leading-carry read below a is an additional source boundary.
