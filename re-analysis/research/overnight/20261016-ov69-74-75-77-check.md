# Opus check of OV69, OV74, OV75 and OV77 (M8-026, M12-047, M12-048, M13-038), 2026-10-16

- **Checked:** the four research answers, against libcozmoEngine.so (Thumb, capstone with skipdata). Read-only
  cozmo-extractor. All are accurate in what they read.
- **Adopted:** by the manager on 2026-10-16.

## Manager decisions

1. **M8-026 (ProcessClearQuad) moves to IMPLEMENTATION_GAP** (job B-M8-8).
   - **It depends on** M11-066 (GetWithRespectTo, which can log) and M11-065 (ApplyTo). The callers belong to M11-074.
   - **The Viz output is unobservable,** because both send sinks are `bx lr`.
2. **M12-047 is split.**
   - **IMPLEMENTATION_GAP:**
     - the helper-stop rows (the wrapper, the component match, the clear loop, IHelper::Stop, LogStopEvent, and the seven
       no-op slots);
     - the BuildPyramid list identity and loop.
   - **Referenced only:** the SmartDelegate rows belong to M8-011 and the StopActing rows to M8-001.
   - **A new RECOVERABLE_GAP:** the BuildPyramid success and failure callbacks (vtables 0x01029068 and 0x010290A8), and
     the pyramid cache contents (Container::Update 0x0061682C onwards, CastNewEntryToBestSharedPointer 0x0062C68C,
     PruneFullPyramids 0x0062C72E, BuildAllPyramidBasesForBlock, PyramidBase::operator==).
   - **The empty-stack read** at 0x0056DCC6 stays an unknown-reachability M1-034 point.
3. **M12-048 has no reachable fault.**
   - **DriveAndFlip's −1 is disproved:** the selector overwrites the index with the count at 0x00550C4E, and the
     consumer returns on any nonzero status at 0x0055E4DA.
   - **A Bridge can't be constructed** in the shipped app: its only constructor callers are Bridge's own Clone
     functions.
   - **So M12-048 stays RECOVERABLE_GAP with live_path false,** following the precedent for unreachable paths. The low
     residual, whether another class could carry ObjectType 10 or 11, is accepted.
   - **B-M12-5's MISSING at 0x0055E59C** can be resolved through M12-035.
4. **M13-038 moves to IMPLEMENTATION_GAP** (job B-M13-8, which also builds the M13-039 handler that was waiting on it).
   - **The behaviour:**
     - two clients per Robot, with A subscribed before B;
     - the broadcaster subscription is unconditional;
     - there's no unsubscribe before the Robot destructor;
     - emission is synchronous and recursive, with snapshots;
     - the stage-duplicate warning.
   - **The nested unlock-0x1D case makes three Activate calls (A twice, B once).** That's defined behaviour, so it's
     reproduced, not suppressed.
   - **Activation needs SetSparkedMusicState (G2E 0xA3).** Whether the stack exposes it is still an operator question.
5. **M8-011:** LogStopEvent's layout is no longer guessed. It's settled by 0x005B6850..0x005B6ACC.
6. **M8-001 lead** (not adopted until M8-001 is re-read): +0x84 is cleared after Cancel whenever it still equals the
   original tag, whatever the first bool (0x005BD3F4..0x005BD3FE). The first bool only adds a clear before Cancel
   (0x005BD3E0..0x005BD3E4).
