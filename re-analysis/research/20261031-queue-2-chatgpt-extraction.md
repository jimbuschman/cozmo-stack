### Direct-access search coverage and object-offset exclusions

Disassembled all of `.text` in **Thumb mode** (native executable text section `0x004D6860...`), searched for memory operands with immediate `#0x15c`, then matched each candidate to its containing exported function and checked its register base. Six relevant instructions were identified:

- **Writer** `0x005DCA88` — `BehaviorBuildPyramidBase` constructor; zero.
- **Reader** `0x005DBF0A` — derived `BehaviorBuildPyramid::InitInternal`.
- **Writers** `0x005DBF20` and `0x005DBF2E` — derived `InitInternal`; zero.
- **Writer** `0x005DC814` — captured derived pointer in the helper callback; one.
- **Reader** `0x005DD100` — inherited base `UpdateInternal`.

The global scan also found many unrelated direct `+0x15C` operands, including at `0x005D10E4` (`BehaviorFactoryTest`) and `0x005E1E82` (`BehaviorExploreLookAroundInPlace`): **these must not be attributed to `BehaviorBuildPyramid` merely because the immediate matches**. The six relevant instruction sites comprise four writes and two reads.

**UNKNOWN:** whether there are additional writes or reads via `this + calculated offset`, aliases, memcpy, indirect access, externally loaded code, or ARM-mode instructions not expressed as direct `#0x15c` operands. A literal operand search cannot prove their absence; no global pointer-alias analysis was performed. No claim is made that all possible runtime accesses have been exhausted.

### Observable consequence and boundary

Native evidence establishes a one-byte latch: constructed `0`; a particular failed-helper callback sets it to `1` if the robot's carrying-ID sentinel is `-1`; derived initialization consumes/clears the latch, optionally entering `TransitionToReactingToPyramid` when the complete-pyramid list is nonempty; inherited update checks the latch plus that list and may stop the behavior with return `2` without clearing it. **That interpretation is limited to the quoted instructions.** Whether the downstream transition produces a particular robot message or action is not established here.

**Contradictions to existing statuses:** Not evaluated; no status change. **Next queue item:** **2 — producer and ordering of the full-pyramid list passed to `PruneFullPyramids(0x0062C72E)`**.
