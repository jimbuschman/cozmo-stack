## 0. Answer in two lines
- event_volume (0xD2687048; ActorMixers 62050212 and 682998829 in Cozmo.bnk, 121198006 in Dev_Debug; param 0) DOES reach every Sound PBI under those roots: 2206 of 2231 Cozmo.bnk Sounds and 12 of 14 Dev_Debug Sounds. The PBI registers at holder node+0x10 (registry [node+0x14]) of the root ActorMixer with mask bit 0. It does not reach the 25 Switch-rooted Cozmo.bnk Sounds (20 under Switch 677877281, 5 under Switch 229678261), the SFX and UI Sounds, or the 2 Dev_Debug SFX-bus Sounds (no event_volume subscription in their chains).
- robot_volume (0x637C1240, Bus 1723505802, param 5) does NOT reach any Sound PBI in the shipped shapes: 0x9C54E8(first output bus) is 1 for both first buses that lead to Cozmo_Robot, which ORs 0x20 into the satisfied mask (0x9F8274..0x9F8288); that removes mask bit 5 before the bus holder test (x = M & A = 0), so the listener is never inserted and the subscription stays in array B.

## 5. Which holders a Sound PBI registers at, shipped shapes (own walk of the banks; Sound chains by directParent; per-node RTPC lists)
Subscription creation: the bank loader's node InitialRTPC reader calls vt+0xF0 with the varint paramId as stack arg 0 (0x9F7254..0x9F72EC); vt+0xF0 = 0x9F1DE0 in every node vtable and in the Bus vtable (0x103ADD0), which calls 0xA1A338(node+0x10, ...) (0x9F1DEC..0x9F1E14); the bus reader does the same (0x9C4278..0x9C42A0). So every bank RTPC (node or bus) lives at holder 1 of its object, in array B (dormant: [registry+0x14]==0 at 0xA1A3D4..0xA1A3E4), with mask-A bit = paramId.

Bits reachable: in the node loop M always contains 0x127DF & L = 0x279D = params 0, 2, 3, 4, 7, 8, 9, 10, 13 (the top node additionally m-prime); in the bus loop M always contains params 0..5 minus what satisfied removed. Every shipped node RTPC has param 0, 2 or 3.
