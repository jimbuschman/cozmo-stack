## Policy and interpretation

AGENTS.md ADP-1 explicitly keeps “switches, states and RTPC values”, “start, stop, seek and pause, and all timing and scheduling”, “WEM decoding (Vorbis and ADPCM)” and “MIDI, note and singing decisions” exact. Its arithmetic exception covers mixing, gain application, LPF/HPF/biquad, EQ, compressor/limiter bodies and resampling, plus NEON lane order and phone libm rounding. It does not exempt an arithmetic function just because it uses floats, NEON or log/pow.

- **KEEP:** extract/check/build the exact behavior; includes pure arithmetic inside the explicitly exact WEM decoder, or arithmetic determining RNG choices, RTPC values, audibility, note times or classifier decisions.
- **SIMPLIFY:** stop instruction-level DSP transliteration; reproduce the exact control/parameter/geometry contract and validate equivalent PCM against the native reference.
- **VERIFY:** establish the control-versus-sample boundary and consumers first. It authorizes no approximate implementation yet.
- **DROP:** stop only the named pure PCM arithmetic research obligation. The same record's controls and its equivalence-test obligation survive.

Each address below is an ELF virtual address, not a file offset. Single entries identify the enclosing function; the range appendix resolves their symbol extents where available. A cited enclosing span does **not** mean every instruction in it may be dropped: DROP is restricted to its stated arithmetic subset. UNKNOWN is used where the record supplies no native endpoint. Configuration constants, ramp endpoints/segment times, stream positions and masks must retain exact widths/bits. No DSP equivalence thresholds are invented here: ADP-1 requires structural checks first, then measured RMS/SNR and maximum error over a path-covering corpus, with thresholds written and approved before enforcement. B-ADP-HARNESS is a separate build job and is not executed by this task.

WEM decoder IMDCT/window NEON is **KEEP**, despite being sample arithmetic, because decoding is explicitly exact. LFO/envelope/RTPC evaluation is **KEEP** when it produces a control value; the unknown PCM consumer is **VERIFY**. Cube shake HPF is **KEEP** because its output chooses a reaction/RTPC. Playback max-instances limiter is **KEEP**; peak-limiter PCM inner math is **SIMPLIFY**. Metering is **VERIFY** until both decision and callback consumers are established. These boundaries prevent the biggest accidental bulk drops.
