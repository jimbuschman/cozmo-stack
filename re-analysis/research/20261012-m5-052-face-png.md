### Linkage and version

The version string passed at `0x0001724E` is the NUL-terminated string `1.6.19` at `0x000C27F9`; the independent
embedded copyright/version banner begins at `0x000C4179` and says libpng 1.6.19, November 12, 2015.
The decoder calls internal libpng bodies directly, not libpng imports. ELF dependencies are
`libopencv_imgproc.so`, `libdl.so`, `liblog.so`, `libz.so`, `libopencv_core.so`, `libtbb.so`, `libm.so`,
`libc++_shared.so`, `libstdc++.so`, `libc.so`; there is no libpng dependency. Thus PNG parsing/transforms are shipped
inside this library; the DEFLATE service is the phone's imported zlib.

### 1-bit values, palette and transparency

The grayscale expansion body is `0x000565EC`. At `0x00056610` it selects bit-depth 1. Its loop shifts the packed
source byte, then `0x0005664E: sbfx r6,r6,#0,#1` sign-extends one bit: 0 becomes integer 0 and 1 becomes integer -1.
`0x00056656: strb r6,[r8,#-1]!` writes 0 or 255. It updates bit depth to 8 at `0x000566FA..0x00056700`.
The transform dispatcher calls this body at `0x000580B0`.

An optional PLTE does not redefine samples of a colour-type-0 grayscale PNG. The native synthetic probe confirms
that it is ignored with the warning `PLTE: ignored in grayscale PNG`. In contrast, colour-type-3 samples are indices:
palette expansion reads each palette RGB triplet at `0x00058066..0x00058088`, before conversion to gray. A two-entry
red/green indexed fixture produces **76/149**, not 0/255. That is a different source colour type, absent from the
shipped face set.

The one-channel path strips alpha, including alpha originating from palette tRNS, and requests no background
composition. A transparent gray sample retains its gray value; a transparent palette entry retains its converted
RGB intensity. The paired synthetic tRNS probes below confirm these cases. This does not assert behavior for every
malformed PLTE/tRNS combination.

### RGB coefficients and rounding (supplemental, no shipped RGB/RGBA)

OpenCV passes R=.299, G=.587, not libpng's default coefficients. `0x00057434..0x0005746A` converts each double to
fixed-point using `0x00050F78` (`floor(value*100000 + .5)`), yielding 29900 and 58700. The setter at
`0x000573CC..0x000573EE` truncates their conversion to 15-bit coefficients: R=9797, G=19234.
At `0x00058100..0x00058112`, B is the remainder `32768-R-G` = 3737.

For 8-bit RGB without gamma tables, `0x000581AA..0x000581E8` computes
`(9797*R + 19234*G + 3737*B) >> 15`, **truncating**, with no rounding addition. Equal R/G/B is copied directly.
This explains the probe's green=149. The separate gamma-table branch adds `0x4000` at `0x00058166`; it is not
the no-metadata path. General gamma-bearing RGB/RGBA behavior and the full 16-bit RGB conversion are outside
the shipped-face question and were not fully extracted here.
