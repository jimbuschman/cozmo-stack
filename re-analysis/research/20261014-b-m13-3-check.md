   OldPlanUnsafe block was moved out of the force = false branch.
   - **What the native does:** StartPlanning stores `force` at +0xA1 (0x004FEB7E).
     - With force = 1, it clears the actions, sets r4 = 0, and jumps to 0x004FECDA (0x004FEBB4..0x004FEBD2). It never
       reaches PlanIsSafe, which is called at 0x004FF424 only when +0xA1 == 0.
     - After the assignment it reloads +0xA1 and does `cbnz` to 0x004FED4C (0x004FECFA..0x004FECFE), which skips the
       OldPlanUnsafe Info at 0x004FED26.
   - **Why it's live:** every drive comes through ComputePathHelper with force = 1 (0x004FD32E).
   - **Fix:** with force = true, skip PlanIsSafe and OldPlanUnsafe. Keep the force = false behaviour (ForcePlan, or a null
     or empty total) as built.
