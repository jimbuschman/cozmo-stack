## The Codex trial

**The verdict: markedly better than the 2026-09-29 baseline,** when 84 of 239 settled records failed. Engine floats
are written as exact bits, all arithmetic stays binary32, and each VFP condition's NaN semantics are carried into the
C#. Gates and order hold throughout, including about 60 destructor sites. The tests use emulator fixtures, not values
from the code. Nothing was invented.

**The weaknesses:**
- It builds to checked rows without reopening their call targets, so a wrong row passes straight through (D1).
- One log-level slip.
- Too much "MISSING:" logging on live paths, including for things that aren't gaps.
- Edge-branch tests are left unwritten.
