## Contradictions with the current record text

- G8's offsets were deliberately labelled UNKNOWN. No prior behavioral claim is contradicted; the new source facts replace those offset labels.
- The older shorthand “on-treads/off-treads flag” for `+0x355` is too weak: it is the full seven-valued `OffTreadsState` enum.

## Weak evidence

- The symbol for 0x0055B554 was stripped. Its constructed name, base action type, predicate storage and float storage are instruction-exact; calling it the `WaitForLambda` constructor is based on the literal and vtable behavior rather than a surviving function symbol.
- The 0x005C0CA8 caller list includes only instruction-proven direct calls. No indirect vtable dispatch to this non-virtual helper was found.

## Open questions

- None for M7-021. The vendor/firmware production of `RobotState` accel/status/lift fields is outside the stated firmware boundary; the engine-side message fields, units and derivation are established.
