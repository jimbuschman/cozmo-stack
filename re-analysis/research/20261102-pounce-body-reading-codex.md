### Finger/result and shared completion details

IsFingerCaught is **pitch delta >0.02 radians OR lift height >35.5**, both strict; pitch delta is f32(currentPitch - savedPitch). It does not require both. Calls to GetPitchAngle/GetLiftHeight and their live producers are imports. Primary: `5F8CF2..5F8D20` establishes/subtracts baseline; `5F8D90..5F8DBE` compares and ORs; literals `5F8E48=3CA3D70A` and `5F8E4C=420E0000`. The lambda is not just the final supplied method: it inserts the lift-power-off/0.15s/lift-power-on sequence in state10. It is used for initial, random-turn and positive pounces alike.

The member-callback adapter `5F8E50..5F8E78` retains the target behavior/member pair and passes a Robot callback to shared StartActing; `5FA216..5FA22A` performs member-pointer dispatch. Those own callbacks do not inspect action result codes. This alone does not establish when a rejected action, exception or synchronous cancellation invokes them.

### Disposition

**Four concrete ordinary-path differences** are established: doubled pose radius, the extra initial-search head action, initial reaction ignoring a retained pitch baseline, and omitted post-pounce creep-count reset (D1–D4). Additional configurable/flag omissions and unverified shared boundaries are listed separately; D7 is not promoted from an internal store difference to a behavioral defect without an observable consequence.

No new compatibility departure is authorized or claimed. Project rules already permit alternate internal mechanisms and sensible host failure handling; they do not make the extra readiness holds, an ignored supported config value, or unverified shared helpers exact. The current `M15-107` **IMPLEMENTATION_GAP** status remains appropriate; no record or inventory was changed. Part2 is a source/code comparison report, not a whole-path fidelity, hardware or acceptance verdict.
