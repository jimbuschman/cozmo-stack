### Finger/result and shared completion details

IsFingerCaught is **pitch delta >0.02 radians OR lift height >35.5**, both strict; pitch delta is f32(currentPitch - savedPitch). It does not require both. Calls to GetPitchAngle/GetLiftHeight and their live producers are imports. Primary: `5F8CF2..5F8D20` establishes/subtracts baseline; `5F8D90..5F8DBE` compares and ORs; literals `5F8E48=3CA3D70A` and `5F8E4C=420E0000`. InitialReaction uses the same helper, even though the first wrapper did not itself refresh the pitch baseline; constructor initializes baseline0 (`5F7FCE`).

PounceOnMotionWithCallback (`5F8A80`) creates a sequential compound, adds the optional first action if nonnull (`5F8AA6..5F8ABA`), then a lift-safe trigger196 (`5F8ACE..5F8AEA`), starts it with its own lambda (`5F8B02..5F8B1E`), and resets creep count0 (`5F8B36..38`). The lambda is not just the final supplied method: it inserts the lift-power-off/0.15s/lift-power-on sequence in state10. It is used for initial, random-turn and positive pounces alike.

The member-callback adapter `5F8E50..5F8E78` retains the target behavior/member pair and passes a Robot callback to shared StartActing; `5FA216..5FA22A` performs member-pointer dispatch. Those own callbacks do not inspect action result codes. This alone does not establish when a rejected action, exception or synchronous cancellation invokes them.

For ending, independently checked shared native instructions are narrow: `5BD074..5BD08A` delegates Update to vtable+0xC unless `+A0` and no action tag; inherited UpdateInternal `5BDA56..5BDA62` returns1 for a nonzero tag+84, otherwise2. In matching HandleActionComplete, `5BE1EC..5BE208` clears tag+84 **before** ScoredActingStateChanged(false), then `5BE20A..5BE220` only invokes stored completion if running byte+A1 and callback+98 remain nonzero. Full cancellation/queue/broadcast and thunk `8CBF5C` are UNKNOWN here (M15-069/M8-015). This behavior's no-callback GetOut action leaves no own repeat transition; the callback chain after every positive result does.
