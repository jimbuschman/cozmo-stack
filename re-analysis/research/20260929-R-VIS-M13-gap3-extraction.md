### OpenCV `minAreaRect` 0x0009C760 (libopencv_imgproc.so, Thumb; it inlines `rotatingCalipers`)

- **hull.** `convexHull(points, hull, clockwise=1, returnPoints=1)` (0x0009C7DC `movs r2,#1`, 0x0009C7E4 `mov r3,r2`, 0x0009C7EE `bl 0x39174`). clockwise is TRUE.
- **depth.** If the hull depth is not CV_32F it is converted (0x0009C7F2..0x0009C8BC); not taken for float input.
- **n.** `n = hull.checkVector(2)`; `n>2` calipers; `n==2` handled at 0x0009CDFE; `n==1` at 0x0009CE82 (0x0009C8CA `cmp r0,#2 / ble.w 0x9cdfc`).
