# M14-011 — FaceRecognizer matching, registration, update and merge

Role: independent `cozmo-extractor`. Date: 2026-09-29. Primary source is the shipped `resources/lib/armeabi-v7a/libcozmoEngine.so`; Ghidra was used only for navigation and the citations below are instruction addresses in that binary.

## Address correction

The request's `RecognizeFace` range is correct, but most requested helper addresses do not name those helpers in this binary. For example, 0x00862808 is destructor cleanup, not `RegisterNewUser`. The symbol-backed bodies actually present in the requested `.so` are: `RegisterNewUser` 0x00865658..0x008657A8, `GetFaceIDforAlbumEntry` 0x00866C1C..0x00866CAC, `UpdateExistingAlbumEntry` 0x00865A98..0x0086600E, the two `RemoveUser` overloads 0x00865544..0x008655C6 and 0x00866AE0..0x00866B9A, `EnrolledFaceEntry::MergeWith` 0x008616AE..0x0086173A, and `ConvertToEnrolledFaceStorage` 0x00860D7C..0x00860EE0. `VisionSystem::SetSerializedFaceData` is at 0x006B9D82. This report cites those actual bodies; treating the stale addresses as evidence would attribute unrelated instructions to M14-011.
