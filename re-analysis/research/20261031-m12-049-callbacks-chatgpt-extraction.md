## Explicit UNKNOWNs, and source-boundary limitations

- The precise virtual implementation reached through the sequential action's `vtable + 0x20` at `0x005DC8D2` and later invocations was not resolved from a proven live vptr. The three supplied action pointers and both zero flags **were** verified.
- There is no verified trigger matrix for exception landing pads `0x005DC9A6–0x005DCA0E`. These pads do not constitute normal behavior paths.
- Detailed robot traffic/message payloads downstream of `BehaviorObjectiveAchieved`, `NeedActionCompleted`, `StartActing`, and action execution are outside the two callback operator bodies and not established here. No direct CLAD message is constructed inside either operator.
- This report does not assert that the registered callbacks always execute: the helper dispatch has stack-state, return-code, and optional-function gates (`0x0056E0FA–0x0056E140`), and `SmartDelegateToHelper`'s return is ignored by the placement loop (`0x005DC298–0x005DC2FE`).
