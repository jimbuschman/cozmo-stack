## Circular, implementation-shaped, or missing tests

The following are blocking as fidelity evidence even where they remain useful regression tests:

1. **M12-001:** `ManipulationTests.cs:81` repeats `56.5771`; `:138-141` recomputes the threshold in the same double arithmetic. Both mask the binary32 defects.
2. **M12-012:** `RestingFlatTests.cs:34-36` converts the implementation decimal to degrees and accepts it loosely; it cannot distinguish `0x3E32B8C2` from `0x3E32B8C7`.
3. **M13-028:** `M12RVisBuildTests.cs:2392-2417` reports 111 mm instead of 92 mm and shares one lock owner, so its two strongest claims are unobserved. This is circular/weak, not merely incomplete coverage.
4. **M11-043, M11-040, M12-026, M12-029, M13-020 and M13-022:** tests call isolated helpers/classes with no production caller. They can verify local mechanics but are circular for the CHECKLIST production-entry requirement.
5. **M12-028, M12-036, M12-039 and M13-007:** tests explicitly assert retained stand-ins or visible throws. Those are useful gap-visibility tests, not evidence that the engine behavior was reproduced.
6. **Missing boundary tests:** M11-004 has no `0x3E32B8C2` ULP threshold test; M12-004 has no one-pose retry; M12-017 has no failed-start/no-squint or unsolicited-0xC5 lifetime test; M12-022 has no native binary32 angle boundary.
