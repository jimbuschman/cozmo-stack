## 0. Conventions and address corrections

### Logger conventions
- **Signatures** are as in `20261011-m3m4-fix-rows.md`:
  - `sChanneledInfoF` and `sChanneledDebugF` take `(channel, key, kv, fmt, ...)`.
  - `sWarningF`, `sErrorF` and `sEventF` take `(key, kv, fmt, ...)`.
- **kv** is empty at every site below.
- **errG/break** is the tail `strb #1 -> _errG`, then `sDebugBreakOnError()` if `_errBreakOnError` is set.
- **`sVerifyFailedReturnFalse(key, fmt, ...)`** (0x0080D9A4) does four things, then returns 0:
  1. `sErrorV(key, {}, fmt, va)` at 0x0080D9C2;
  2. `_errG = 1` at 0x0080D9F4. It does **not** call `sDebugBreakOnError`;
  3. `sDumpCallstack("VERIFY")` at 0x0080DA08;
  4. `gLoggerProvider->vtbl+0x14` (flush) at 0x0080DA24.
- **Arguments** are listed in printf order. "name" means the C string of a `std::string`.
- **Channel "Unnamed"** is the string at 0x00BE3FEC, unless a local copy is cited.
- **Reach:** per M3-048 (m3m4 rows §7), shipped DASConfig drops info and debug, so only warnings and errors reach a sink. That does not change the rows.
