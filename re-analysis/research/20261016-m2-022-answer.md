# Opus answer: M2-022 (local E2G 0x60 subscribers; nesting inside Clear), 2026-10-16

- **Method:** a read-only Opus extractor (Thumb, capstone with skipdata). It ran a type-resolved census of all 48
  E2G callback types through their typeinfo names, the creation site of each, and the tag sets decoded from every
  Subscribe path. Scratch files are in `r2022\`.
- **Adopted:** by the manager.

**Conclusions:**
- **Tag 0x60:** no engine-local subscriber. AnimationEvent only goes to the game.
- **Nesting:** a robot-message dispatch never runs nested inside ActionList::Clear or ActionQueue::Clear, and Clear
  never runs inside a robot-message emit.

**Residuals:**
- **R1:** an E2G callable without RTTI would be missed by the census; none is known.
- **R2:** an indirect call to ProcessMessages through some other MessageHandler* path isn't excluded by construction.
- **R3:** an inline call to a RobotToEngine std::function outside the emit wasn't scanned for.
