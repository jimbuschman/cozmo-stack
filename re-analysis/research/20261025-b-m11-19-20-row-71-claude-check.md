The answer's app-side and shipped-library searches are taken as reported (authority 2/3 corpus, bounded).

**Manager decision (2026-10-25), recorded under AGENTS.md "What we reproduce" (paths our system cannot reach):**
- **What:** VisionComponent::FakeImageProcessed (0x00657C24, the +0xE8 store at 0x00657C2E) is **not supported**.
- **Why:**
  - nothing in the shipped engine calls it (no branch, relocation or pointer);
  - nothing in the shipped app or libraries imports or names it;
  - the stack replaces the app and gives it no caller.
- **Scope:** this is not a "no effect" claim. The function does write +0xE8 if called, and the decision is reversible
  with this evidence.
- **Where it gets recorded:** in M11-035's `unresolved`, when B-M11-21 is accepted.

So the stack's VisionComponent+0xE8 has three writers:
- the constructor (0);
- the SetNextImage rollback (0, with +0xE4);
- the result tail after the handlers.
