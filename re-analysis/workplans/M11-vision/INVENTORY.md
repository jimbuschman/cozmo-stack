This is the proposed replacement for the legacy M11-001..M11-020 list. It is deliberately more granular: an exact constant inside a locally invented pipeline is not evidence for that pipeline. The implementation must not begin until the manager reconciles these rows with `fidelity_manifest.json`, writes the approved inventory, and obtains the Process checkpoint.

## Scope boundaries

- M3 owns image transport, JPEG/YUV decoding, camera parameter messages, and NV calibration storage. M11 begins when a decoded frame is admitted to `VisionComponent`.
- M4 owns cube radio events and direct motion commands. M11 consumes connected-object state and produces world observations.
- M14 owns face identity and the OKAO-backed face/pet implementations. M11 owns only their scheduling and result-delivery boundary.
- The memory-map effects produced from M11 observations are included because they change what navigation and behavior consumers see.
