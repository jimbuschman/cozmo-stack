# M11 acceptance design

## Gate before implementation

M11 cannot enter implementation until:

1. The manager integrates this candidate into `re-analysis/inventory/M11-vision.md` and the manifest without overwriting active M6 work.
2. A read-only extractor closes M11-027, M11-028, and M11-031 or establishes a cited scope boundary.
3. Every settled record cites an address or shipped file, every claim is narrowed to what that evidence proves, and the operator approves the inventory.
4. `python re-analysis/tools/fidelity.py --approve M11-vision` freezes the approved evidence.

## Automated tests required

### Frame and history

- Native-oracle vectors for interpolation before, between, and after raw states; origin change; timestamp wrap; missing state; and culling boundaries.
- Admission tests for uninitialized/no-calibration, non-increasing timestamps, busy worker, stop/restart, and result-list lifetime.
- A schedule table test generated from shipped config: marker/face/motion cadence, alternating pet/overhead frames, quality every 15, laser every two, and disabled statistics behavior.
- A frame-order test proving the detector and publication order at one shared timestamp.

### Marker pipeline

- Preserve the exhaustive 598-code library/rotation decoder test.
- Add native-output fixtures for real and adversarial grayscale crops: low contrast, border-touching, two adjacent markers, blur, skew, duplicate candidates, and candidate-capacity pressure.
- Golden intermediate data for characteristic scale, smoothing, connected components, traced boundary, peak lines, quad corners, refined corners, and final code. This prevents the final decoder from hiding an earlier mismatch.
- CLAHE on/off and rolling-shutter-shift vectors from native outputs.

### Geometry and pose

- Native `solvePnP` golden vectors covering fronto-parallel, oblique, near-degenerate, distorted, initialized-estimate, and failure cases. Assert success/failure and pose tolerances against native output, not a managed re-projection generated by the same model.
- Camera pose vectors with body yaw plus nonzero pitch/head/lift and calibrated distortion.
- Canonical cube and charger marker transforms checked independently against native data.

### BlockWorld

- Two-sighting confirmation and miss sequences with assertions after every frame.
- Connected cube association, wrong-type marker, two same-type cubes, disconnect/reconnect, and ID reuse.
- Occlusion fixtures for image bounds, back-facing markers, another object, body, and lift.
- Carried-cube moved event, stopped event, dirty/known transitions, and post-release behavior.
- Delocalization fixtures with carried, observed, unobserved, and orphan-origin objects plus map content.
- No local RMS, ID, or capacity policy may be used as an oracle until it is classified.

### Overhead, motion, and quality

- Native-output overhead edge fixtures for flat ground, border, short/noisy runs, raised lift, and invalid ground plane; verify map polygons and timestamps.
- Native motion masks/regions for the shipped settings and schedule.
- Image-quality classifications and exposure-feedback outputs at schedule boundaries, with M3 message assertions.

## Regression suite treatment

Existing synthetic and exhaustive tests are retained. They are useful regressions but must be labeled by what they prove; none is deleted or weakened. Tests using self-rendered markers or self-projected pose data remain regression tests, while native-oracle fixtures supply fidelity evidence.

## Hardware plan

Only the operator runs hardware. After offline acceptance, add one self-judging `M11-VISION` script that writes a bundle under `re-analysis/acceptance/hardware/<timestamp>-M11-VISION/` containing machine-readable results, logs, captured frames, robot-state samples, camera calibration, script hash, and relevant fidelity IDs.

Physical setup:

1. Calibrated robot, one cube of each type, charger, matte floor, printed official markers, and a fixed measurement grid.
2. Repeatable light levels (bright, normal, dim) and a fixed camera/target geometry.
3. A lift-occlusion placement, an oblique marker placement, and a short drive producing nonzero body pitch/motion.

Checks:

- calibration is loaded from the M3 NV path before frames are accepted;
- observed cube type/ID/pose and two-sighting timing;
- cube hidden by the lift is not falsely missed/forgotten;
- move/carry/release and disconnect/reconnect lifecycle;
- charger pose and charger-platform clearing after driving off;
- overhead border/map result;
- scheduled mode cadence and dropped/busy-frame accounting;
- captured original-app/native comparison for at least pose, marker result, motion, and quality/exposure output.

Hardware-only uncertainty must be listed in the bundle before the run. A pass verifies the physical integration only; it does not reclassify source provenance.

## Final acceptance gate

- All approved M11 records are tagged and the fidelity checker passes.
- The adversarial verifier finds no unsupported/contradicted behavior, circular fidelity tests, races, or deadlocks.
- Focused M11 tests pass, then the full suite passes once immediately before commit.
- `PROJECT_STATE.md` records the accepted commit and every residual uncertainty by fidelity ID.
